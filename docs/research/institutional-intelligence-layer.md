# Market-intelligence research registry

This public note preserves the identifiers required to reproduce the committed research-spec lifecycle without publishing the project's internal working design document.

The market-intelligence layer is advisory research context only. It may enrich or veto a candidate decision, but it does not bypass validation, deterministic risk or execution controls.

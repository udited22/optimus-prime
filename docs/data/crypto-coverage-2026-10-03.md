## Notes, biases and limits

- Run status: **SNAPSHOT: 16,169 files were still pending when this report was generated**; re-run `scripts/crypto_backfill.py run` (it resumes) and then `report` to refresh.
- **Survivorship bias.** The universe is TODAY's top 10 by market cap (CoinLore snapshot). It was not the top 10 in earlier years (e.g. LUNA, DOT, MATIC, AVAX, ADA, BCH and LTC ranked high in 2018-2022; HYPE did not exist). A backtest over this list overstates what a point-in-time strategy could have picked. No free, keyless point-in-time market-cap membership history was found; a fair test needs one (e.g. a paid historical-rank feed) or a rule-based universe built from Binance volume itself.
- Binance-only coverage: pairs delisted from Binance (XMR spot, 20-Feb-2024) end there even though the asset still trades elsewhere; prices are Binance USDT prices, not a consolidated index.
- The USD-M archive starts 2019-12-31/2020-01 even though the BTC perpetual launched in Sep-2019; the metrics archive starts 2020-09 for BTC and 2021-12 for the others.
- Spot Dec-2017 and Feb-2018 monthly files for BTC, ETH and BNB carry bars stamped off the minute (an exchange-side artefact: from 4-Dec-2017 06:00 UTC, and after the 8/9-Feb-2018 outage); they are quarantined, so spot history has holes there. The 'Longest gap' column includes quarantined files.
- Gaps in clean files are real exchange outages or maintenance windows (e.g. 600-minute and 4,320-minute gaps), kept as WARN and never filled.
- The REST API fallback (api.binance.com / fapi.binance.com) is not implemented: both answer HTTP 451 from this box's region, and the archive covers everything up to yesterday (UTC).
- INR prices: CoinDCX publishes public INR-quoted market data and is the candidate if an INR series is ever needed (Indian tax and TDS treatment differs); it is NOT used here.

- Hedged-spread margin for NIFTY credit spreads. Pull it from a broker margin API or the NSE SPAN calculator at the time of use.

- Tax treatment (F&O as non-speculative business income, audit thresholds, the new Income-tax Act "tax year"). Consult a Chartered Accountant.
- Whether exchange cancellation of "special orders (stop-loss)" at the CAS price-range reset applies to index options or only to stock futures (S18 wording refers to stock futures).
